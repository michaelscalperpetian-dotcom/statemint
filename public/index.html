The problem

Airbnb moves the money. It doesn't explain it.

Payouts arrive net of fees, cleaning is mixed in, stays straddle months, and every month you rebuild the same spreadsheet to explain where the money went: to an owner, a co-owner, or yourself at tax time.

📄
Drop in the CSV

Export your transaction history from Airbnb and drop it in. Bank-transfer rows are skipped so nothing is counted twice, and every skipped row is listed in the audit trail.

🧮
Your own home or one you manage

For your own place: payout, fees, cleaning and expenses, done. For a home you manage: commission on payout, payout minus cleaning, or gross, who keeps cleaning fees, and who owes whom. Saved per property.

FAQ

Questions hosts ask

Where is my data saved?

Your CSV, settings, expenses and saved statements are saved to your Mint Statements account so they're there on any device you sign in on. "Delete my saved data" removes them, and you can delete your whole account from your dashboard. If you create an owner link, we store that one statement (the figures, guest names as you chose to show them, your branding and note) and only the email you shared it with can open it, after signing in with Google. You can delete a link at any time.

Where do I get the CSV?

On Airbnb: Menu → Earnings → Transaction history → filter by listing and dates → Export CSV.

I host my own place. Is this for me?

Yes. Mark the property as "My own home" and you get an earnings report instead of an owner statement: no commission, no settlement, just what you made and where it went.

What counts as a property?

One Airbnb listing. Free covers one, Host covers five, Pro is unlimited. If your file has more listings than your plan, you choose which ones are on it.

How does the free trial work?

New Host and Pro subscribers get 7 days free, once per account. You enter a card at checkout and nothing is charged during the trial. If you don't cancel before it ends, the plan starts and renews automatically every month or year until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Go to your dashboard or the statement builder and press Manage subscription, then Cancel. You keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. We don't give refunds for partial periods; see the refund policy.

Airbnb already splits payouts between me and the owner. Does it still help?

Yes. Pick "Airbnb split", enter what Airbnb paid you, and Mint Statements shows whether anyone owes a difference against your agreed deal.

Is this accounting or tax advice?

No. Mint Statements organizes the numbers in your Airbnb report. Check anything tax-related with your accountant.
