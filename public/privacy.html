Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Mint Statements (mintstatements.com) is run by [Company legal name], based in California, USA ("we", "us"). It turns your Airbnb transaction report into owner statements and earnings reports. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Questions or requests: support@mintstatements.com.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address and Google's ID for your account, and nothing else: not your name, photo, password or anything in your Google account.
  • What you put into the app. The Airbnb CSV you upload and what you enter: deals, expenses, notes, branding (including a logo), owners' email addresses and saved statements. This can include guest names and booking details from your Airbnb report. We keep it in your account so it's there on every device you sign in on.
  • Owner links. When you create an owner link we store that one statement (the figures, guest names in the form you chose to show, your branding and note), the owner's email address, and how many times it was opened.
  • Your plan. If you subscribe we store your plan, its status and the IDs Stripe gives your customer record and subscription. We never receive or store card details.
  • Abuse protection. To limit how many owner links can be created from one network, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your IP address for 24 hours, then delete it.
  • Technical data. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, we don't build profiles about you, and we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Why we use it

Only to provide Mint Statements: to sign you in, keep your work in your account, show a statement to the person you shared it with, give you the plan you pay for, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Where EU or UK law applies, our legal bases are performing our contract with you and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Information about other people

Your Airbnb report contains information about your guests, and you may enter owners' email addresses. You're responsible for having the right to upload and share that information. We process it only to provide the service to you.

Who can see a statement you share

Only the email address you entered for the owner, after they sign in with that Google account, and you. Owner links made before sign-in existed aren't tied to an email: anyone signed in who has one of those links can open it. Every owner link stops working 30 days after it's made, and you can delete any link sooner from your dashboard.

Who we share it with

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site and the database where your account data is stored.
  • Stripe and Link, which sell and process subscriptions as the merchant of record, including card details, receipts and sales tax. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect at checkout.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, and a buyer of our business if it's ever sold (they'd be bound by this policy).

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you've saved: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Owner links: until you delete them or your account. They stop opening 30 days after they're made.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days. The scrambled IP address: 24 hours.
  • Plan records: while your account exists. Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: "Delete my saved data" removes your CSV, settings and saved statements; "Delete my account" on your dashboard removes your account and everything stored for it. If you have a subscription, cancel it first.
  • Access, correct or get a copy: email support@mintstatements.com and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • California residents can ask what personal information we hold, ask us to delete or correct it, and won't be treated differently for asking. We don't sell or share personal information.
  • EU and UK residents also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to data portability and to complain to their local data protection authority.

Do Not Track

We don't track you across other websites, so browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals don't change anything here.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to sign you in. See the cookie policy.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in cookies can't be read by page scripts, only scrambled (hashed) copies of session tokens are stored, and each account can only reach its own data. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

Mint Statements is for adults running a hosting business. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Where data is processed

We're in the United States. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell signed-in users about important changes.
