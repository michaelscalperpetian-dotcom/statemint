Ownermint

Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Ownermint turns your Airbnb transaction report into owner statements and earnings reports. This page explains what we store, why, and how you can delete it. Ownermint is not affiliated with Airbnb.

What we store

  • Your Google account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, your email address and Google's ID for your account. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • What you save in the app. The Airbnb CSV you upload and what you enter: deals, expenses, notes, branding (including a logo), owners' email addresses and saved statements. We keep these in your account so they're there on every device you sign in on.
  • Owner links. When you create an owner link we store that one statement: the figures, guest names in the form you chose to show them, your branding and note, the owner's email address, and how many times the link was opened.
  • Your plan. If you subscribe, we store your plan, its status and the Stripe IDs for your customer record and subscription. We never see or store your card details.
  • Abuse protection. To limit how many owner links can be created from one network, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for 24 hours, then delete it.

Why we use it

Only to run Ownermint: to sign you in, keep your work in your account, show a statement to the person you shared it with, and give you the plan you pay for. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and Ownermint has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Who can see a statement you share

Only the email address you entered for the owner, after they sign in with that Google account, and you. Links made before sign-in existed aren't tied to an email: anyone signed in who has one of those links can open it. Every owner link stops working 30 days after it's made, and you can delete any link sooner from your dashboard.

Services we use

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site and the database where your account data is stored.
  • Stripe, which handles payments as the merchant of record, including card details and sales tax. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what it collects at checkout.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the site's fonts; your browser requests them from Google.

Cookies

We use two cookies, both needed for signing in: one that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days (we store only a scrambled version of it), and a short-lived one that protects the Google sign-in step and expires after 10 minutes. There are no tracking or advertising cookies. The app also keeps a copy of your work in your browser's storage so it loads quickly.

Deleting your data

  • Delete my saved data (in the app or on your dashboard) removes your CSV, settings, expenses, notes, branding and saved copies from your account and this browser.
  • Delete an owner link from your dashboard or the Saved tab to take it offline.
  • Delete my account on your dashboard removes your account and everything stored for it, including your owner links. If you have a subscription, cancel it first under Manage subscription; you can delete your account once the paid period has ended. Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires.
  • Sign out clears your work from that browser; it stays in your account.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
