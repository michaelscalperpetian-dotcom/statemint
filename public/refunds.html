Refund policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy covers Host and Pro subscriptions to Mint Statements.

Free trial

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial, once per account. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

Cancel anytime

Cancel under Manage subscription in the app or on your dashboard. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep your plan until the end of the period you've already paid for.

No refunds for partial periods

Payments are non-refundable, and we don't give refunds or credits for unused time in a monthly or yearly period, including when you cancel or switch to a lower plan part-way through.

When we do refund

  • If you were charged by mistake, for example twice for the same period, or after you'd cancelled.
  • If we close the service, for any unused prepaid period.
  • Where the law of the place you live gives you a right to a refund or to withdraw from the purchase.

Purchases are sold through Stripe's Link service as the merchant of record. Link support can also help with a payment and, under its own policies, may issue a refund within 60 days of a charge.

How to ask

Email support@mintstatements.com from the email address you sign in with, and include the date and amount of the charge. We reply within 5 business days.
