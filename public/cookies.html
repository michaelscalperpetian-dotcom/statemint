Cookie policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Mint Statements uses only what's needed to sign you in and run the app. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner to accept.

Cookies

NameWhat it's forHow long
om_sessionKeeps you signed in. Only a scrambled copy is stored on our side, and page scripts can't read it.Up to 30 days, or until you sign out
om_oauthProtects the Google sign-in step from forgery while you sign in.10 minutes

Both are strictly necessary: without them you can't sign in. They're first-party (set by mintstatements.com) and never used for tracking.

Browser storage

The app keeps a copy of your work in your browser's local storage so it loads quickly and keeps working if your connection drops. It's cleared when you sign out.

Other sites

When you sign in, you're briefly on Google's site, and when you subscribe you're on Stripe's checkout. Those sites set their own cookies under their own policies. Mint Statements doesn't load any third-party scripts, fonts or trackers on its own pages.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but you won't be able to sign in without the two above. Questions: support@mintstatements.com.
